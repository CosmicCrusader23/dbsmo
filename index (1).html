HKOI 初賽模擬卷

一份完整的模擬卷，格式、分數和時間都照 2025/26 初級組真卷：卷一 50 分鐘（25 分），卷二（C++）40 分鐘（20 分），兩卷連續做，中間不休息。難度比真卷高一些（容易題較少）；做完會合計兩卷，和近兩屆的晉級線比較。

怎樣做

  1. 預留 95 分鐘，準備紙筆，找一個不會被打擾的地方。只用紙筆計算，不要用電腦試程式。
  2. 按「開始卷一」。卷一交卷後，成績頁有按鈕直接開始卷二。
  3. 兩卷在同一部裝置做，卷二的成績頁才會自動合計兩卷；在不同裝置做的話，可以自己輸入卷一的分數。
  4. 做完才看題解。成績頁會列出各部分的用時、答錯題目的考點和考試策略建議；按題號就到該題的題解。

卷一

甲部是邏輯、數學和數據結構的選擇題；乙部是短答題；丙部兩題分成幾部分（G1、G2 等），全部正確才有分。程式只用 C++ 和 Python 都有的寫法，但只印 C++ 版本（真卷卷一會同時印 Python 和 C++）。

50 分鐘 · 25 分 · 甲部 10 題選擇題、乙部 6 題短答題、丙部 2 題

開始卷一題解

卷二（C++）

C++ 的語言細節（整數除法、else 的配對、引用參數、位元運算、短路求值）、追蹤和寫條件式，以及兩個程式填空。

40 分鐘 · 20 分 · 甲部 5 題選擇題、乙部 6 題短答題、丙部 2 題

開始卷二題解

考試策略（真卷也適用）

  • 選擇題答錯不扣分：交卷前每題都要填上答案。
  • 先做有把握的題目；一題超過建議時間仍沒有頭緒，先跳過，做完其他題目再回來。
  • 卷一交卷後就不能再改，卷一剩下的時間也不能留給卷二用。
  • 答題紙每格只寫一個字元，不可超出格數，寫得不清楚當作錯；分成幾部分的空格要全部正確才有分。
  • 追蹤程式時列出變量表；數很大的次數時，先試小的例子找規律。

做完之後

如果還未做過，可以再做一次 HKOI 官方的 2025/26 初級組真卷，用同樣的方法計時： 2025/26 初級組真卷（PDF，HKOI 官方網站）

網頁不會記下姓名，只會在你的裝置記下一個隨機編號和這兩卷的分數（用來合計兩卷）。

HKOI Heat mock paper

A complete mock paper built like the 2025/26 Junior paper in format, marks and time: Paper 1 takes 50 minutes (25 marks) and Paper 2 (C++) 40 minutes (20 marks), back to back with no break. It is a little harder than the real paper (fewer easy questions); afterwards both papers are added up and compared with the last two cut-offs.

How to do it

  1. Set aside 95 minutes, with paper and pen, somewhere you will not be disturbed. Work on paper only; do not try programs on a computer.
  2. Press "Start Paper 1". After you hand it in, the results page has a button that starts Paper 2 straight away.
  3. Do both papers on the same device so that Paper 2's results page can add them up; on different devices you can type in your Paper 1 score.
  4. Read the explanations only afterwards. The results page shows the time per section, the topics of the questions you missed and advice on exam strategy; tap a question number to open its explanation.

Paper 1

Section A is multiple choice on logic, maths and data structures; Section B has short answers; the two Section C questions are split into parts (G1, G2 and so on) that score only if all are right. Programs use only features that C++ and Python share, but only the C++ version is printed (the real Paper 1 prints both Python and C++).

50 minutes · 25 marks · Section A 10 multiple choice, Section B 6 short answers, Section C 2 questions

Paper 2 (C++)

C++ language details (integer division, matching else, reference parameters, bitwise operators, short-circuit evaluation), tracing and writing conditions, and two programs to complete.

40 minutes · 20 marks · Section A 5 multiple choice, Section B 6 short answers, Section C 2 questions

Start Paper 2Explanations

Exam strategy (for the real paper too)

  • Wrong multiple-choice answers lose no marks: fill in every one before handing in.
  • Do the questions you are sure of first; if you still have no idea after the suggested time, skip it and come back after the others.
  • Once Paper 1 is handed in it cannot be changed, and its spare time cannot be used on Paper 2.
  • On the answer sheet write one character per box, never more boxes than given; unclear writing counts as wrong, and a blank split into parts scores only if every part is right.
  • Trace programs with a table of variables; for very large counts, try small cases first and look for a pattern.

Afterwards

If you have not done it yet, try HKOI's official 2025/26 Junior paper the same way, timed: 2025/26 Junior paper (PDF, HKOI's official site)

The pages never record your name; your device keeps only a random ID and your scores on these two papers (to add them up).
