HKOI 初賽限時練習

三份練習，每份 10 題、約 55 分鐘，難度對準初級組歷年最難的題目（進階篇更難），做完即時對答案。卷二的題目用 C++。

時間等級（在每份練習的開始畫面選）

  • 簡單：真卷時間，即熟練考生做這一題需要的時間。時間到不會跳過，可以繼續作答，只會記錄超時多少。
  • 中等：真卷時間。時間到就跳過：練習模式直接看題解，考試模式整份交卷。
  • 困難：真卷時間的七成，時間到就跳過。要做得很熟練才夠時間。

怎樣用

  1. 選一份，按「開始練習」，再選練習模式或考試模式，以及時間等級。
  2. 練習模式：一題一題做，每題有建議時間，答完立即看答案和解說。考試模式：所選題目一起計時，交卷後才看答案，最接近真實考試。
  3. 交卷後會顯示「成績碼」。如果老師要求，把它交給老師。
  4. 題解頁有全部答案和解說，最好做完才看。

HKOI Heat timed practice

Three practice sets of 10 questions, about 55 minutes each, at the level of the hardest past Junior questions (Advanced goes further), with answers straight after. Paper 2 questions use C++.

Time levels (chosen on each practice's start screen)

  • Easy: The real-exam time, that is, what a well-practised student needs for the question. When time runs out you can keep going; the page only records how far over you went.
  • Medium: The real-exam time. When time runs out the question is skipped: practice mode shows the explanation, and exam mode hands in the whole paper.
  • Hard: 70% of the real-exam time, and time-outs skip as in Medium. You need to be very well practised.

How to use

  1. Pick a set, press "Start practice", then choose practice mode or exam mode and a time level.
  2. Practice mode: one question at a time, each with a suggested time, and you see the answer and explanation straight away. Exam mode: the chosen questions share one time limit, with answers only after you hand in, like the real exam.
  3. After you hand in, a "result code" appears. If your teacher asks, hand it in.
  4. The explanation pages show every answer, so look at them after you finish.
